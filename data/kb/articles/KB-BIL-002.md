# How billing works

## Overview

CloudFlow paid plans (Pro, Business and Enterprise) are billed monthly by card. This article explains when you are charged, which currencies are supported and where to find your invoices. The Free plan is never charged.

## Billing day

Your billing day is the day of the month on which your paid plan started. On that day each month we charge the plan price and issue an invoice.

If you upgrade partway through a period, the upgrade applies immediately and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current period. Downgrades take effect on your next billing day.

## Currencies and payment methods

- Workspaces are billed in **USD** or **INR**.
- We accept card payments. For your security, invoices and billing pages show only the **last four digits** of the card, never the full number.

If you need your invoices in the other supported currency, contact support and we will pass the request to the billing team.

## Finding your invoices

- **CloudFlow 4.x:** open **Admin → Billing → Invoices**.
- **CloudFlow 3.x:** open **Settings → Billing** in the left sidebar.

Each invoice shows its invoice ID (for example INV-1234), the amount, currency, charge date, the card's last four digits and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| paid | The charge succeeded. |
| failed | The charge did not go through. See *Failed payments and past-due accounts* (KB-BIL-004). |
| refunded | The billing team refunded the charge. |

## Common questions

- **I was charged twice.** See *Duplicate charges and billing disputes* (KB-BIL-003).
- **I want a refund.** Refunds follow the *Refund policy* (POL-REFUND-001). See *Requesting a refund* (KB-BIL-006).
- **My card is about to expire.** Update the payment method before your next billing day to avoid a failed payment.

## Applies to

Pro, Business and Enterprise plans (the Free plan has no charges). All product versions; menu paths are given for 4.x and 3.x.
