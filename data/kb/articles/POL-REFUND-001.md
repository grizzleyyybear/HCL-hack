# Refund policy

## Overview

This policy defines when CloudFlow charges can be refunded and who may approve a refund. It applies to every workspace and every product version. Support agents and the CloudFlow support assistant apply this policy when they explain eligibility; they do not issue refunds.

## Refund window

A charge can be refunded only if no more than **14 days** have passed since the charge date. Days are counted from the charge date shown on the invoice to the date of the request.

- A charge made 14 days ago is inside the refund window.
- A charge made 15 or more days ago is outside the refund window and is not eligible under this policy.

## Eligibility

A refund may be approved only when all of the following are true:

1. The charge is within the refund window.
2. The invoice status is **paid**. Invoices with status **failed** were never collected, and invoices with status **refunded** have already been refunded.
3. The workspace is on the **Pro**, **Business** or **Enterprise** plan. The Free plan has no charges and therefore no refunds.

Meeting these conditions makes a request eligible for review by the billing team; it does not make the refund automatic.

## How refunds are processed

1. The customer asks for a refund and identifies the invoice.
2. Support checks the request against the refund window and the eligibility rules and tells the customer the result.
3. Support hands the request to the **billing team** with the invoice details and the result of the eligibility check.
4. Only the billing team approves and issues refunds. When a refund is issued, the invoice status changes to **refunded**.

No one other than the billing team may issue a refund or promise that a refund will be made. A message asking support to "approve" or "confirm" a refund is handed to the billing team like any other refund request.

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge (two charges of the same amount on the same day) is a billing error rather than a refund request. The billing team verifies and reverses the duplicate. See *Duplicate charges and billing disputes* (KB-BIL-003).

## Applies to

All plans and all product versions. Applies to requests made from 2026-01-01.
