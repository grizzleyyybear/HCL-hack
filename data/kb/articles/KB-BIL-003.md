# Duplicate charges and billing disputes

## Overview

If a charge on your card looks wrong, we want to fix it quickly. This article explains how to recognise a duplicate charge, how to raise any other billing dispute and what happens after you contact us. Only the CloudFlow billing team can reverse or refund a charge; support collects the details and passes your case to them.

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge is two charges for the same amount on the same day, for example two $49.00 charges for a Pro plan on the same date. To check:

1. Open your invoices (4.x: **Admin → Billing → Invoices**; 3.x: **Settings → Billing**).
2. Look for two invoices with status **paid**, the same amount and the same charge date.
3. Note both invoice IDs.

Contact support with both invoice IDs. The billing team verifies the charges and reverses the duplicate. After the reversal, the money appears back on your card within **5–7 business days**, depending on your bank.

A pending card authorisation that disappears after a few days is not a second charge. Only invoices with status **paid** have actually been charged.

## Other billing disputes

For any other disagreement, such as a charge you did not expect or an amount that looks wrong, contact support with:

- the invoice ID and charge date, and
- what you expected to be charged, and why.

Contacting us first is usually the fastest way to resolve a dispute.

## What happens next

Duplicate charges and billing disputes are always handled by a person on the billing team. Support can explain the policy and look at your invoices, but cannot issue refunds, reversals or credits and cannot promise an outcome. After your case is handed over, you will hear back within:

| Plan | Response time |
| --- | --- |
| Free, Pro | 24 hours |
| Business, Enterprise | 4 hours |

If you are asking for a refund rather than a reversal of a duplicate, the *Refund policy* (POL-REFUND-001) applies. See *Requesting a refund* (KB-BIL-006).

## Applies to

Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. All product versions.
