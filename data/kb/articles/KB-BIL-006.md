# Requesting a refund

## Overview

This article explains when a CloudFlow charge can be refunded and how to ask for a refund. The binding rules are in the *Refund policy* (POL-REFUND-001); this article is a summary.

## Who is eligible

A charge can be considered for a refund when all of these are true:

- **It is within the refund window:** no more than **14 days** have passed since the charge date. A charge made 14 days ago is still inside the window; a charge made 15 days ago is not.
- **The invoice is paid.** Failed charges were never collected, and refunded invoices have already been refunded.
- **The plan is Pro, Business or Enterprise.** The Free plan has no charges, so there is nothing to refund.

Duplicate charges are handled separately and reversed by the billing team. See *Duplicate charges and billing disputes* (KB-BIL-003).

## How to request a refund

1. Find the invoice (4.x: **Admin → Billing → Invoices**; 3.x: **Settings → Billing**) and note its invoice ID, for example INV-1234.
2. Contact support with the invoice ID and the reason for your request.
3. Support checks the invoice against the refund window and the eligibility rules and tells you the result.
4. Support hands the request to the billing team with the invoice details.

## Who decides

Only the **billing team** approves and issues refunds. Support, including the CloudFlow support assistant, can explain whether a charge appears to be eligible, but cannot issue a refund and cannot promise one. You will hear back from the billing team within 24 hours on Pro, or within 4 hours on Business and Enterprise.

When a refund is issued, the invoice status changes to **refunded**.

## Applies to

Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. All product versions.
