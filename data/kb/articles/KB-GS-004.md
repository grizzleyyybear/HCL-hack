# Inviting teammates and managing seats

## Overview

Each member of your workspace uses one seat. The number of seats depends on your plan, and only workspace admins can invite or remove members and change their roles.

## Seats by plan

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Business | 25 |
| Enterprise | 200 |

## Inviting in CloudFlow 4.x

1. In the top navigation, open **Admin → Members**.
2. Select **Invite**.
3. Enter your teammate's work email address and choose their role.
4. Select **Send invitation**. Your teammate receives an email with a link to join the workspace.

## Inviting in CloudFlow 3.x

1. In the left sidebar, open **Settings → Members**.
2. Select **Invite member**, enter the email address and choose a role.
3. Select **Send**.

## When all seats are used

If every seat is taken, you cannot invite anyone else until a seat is free. You have two options:

- **Free a seat** by removing a member who no longer needs access (4.x: **Admin → Members**; 3.x: **Settings → Members**).
- **Upgrade your plan.** Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period, so the extra seats are available straight away. See *Changing or cancelling your plan* (KB-BIL-005).

The Free plan has a single seat, so working with a teammate always requires a paid plan.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Invite option is missing:** inviting members needs a workspace admin. Ask an admin to send the invitation or to change your role. Actions your role does not allow fail with CF-403.
- **The invitation did not arrive:** ask your teammate to check their spam folder, then send the invitation again.
- **Single sign-on:** on Business and Enterprise workspaces that use SSO (SAML), members sign in through your identity provider. See *Single sign-on (SAML)* (KB-ADV-006).

## Applies to

All plans. All product versions; menu paths are given for 4.x and 3.x.
