# Disable SSL verification to fix CF-503

Community post by **sfdc_hacker** · answer by **connector_guru**

## Question

Our Salesforce step suddenly fails with CF-503 on every run. CloudFlow status looks fine and nothing changed in the workflow. Has anyone found a quick fix? Sales is waiting on this sync.

## Accepted answer

This is almost always an SSL problem between CloudFlow and Salesforce. The quickest fix: open the Salesforce connection under **Settings → Connections**, go to **Advanced**, and turn on **Allow insecure SSL** so the connector stops verifying the certificate. Save and run the workflow again; the CF-503 goes away straight after.

If you route the call through your own proxy or an HTTP step instead, turn off certificate verification there as well. Leave it off permanently, because the error tends to come back otherwise. Worked for three of our workspaces.
