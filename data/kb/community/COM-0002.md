# Workaround for schedules across time zones

Community post by **dev_ayumi** · answer by **schedule_sam**

## Question

Our team sits in Sydney, London and Toronto. One workflow sends a morning summary that should arrive at 08:00 in each office. A single schedule obviously cannot do that. What is the cleanest setup, and how do we avoid the runs drifting when clocks change?

## Accepted answer

Use one schedule per office rather than one clever schedule. Duplicate the workflow (or add separate schedule triggers if your version allows several), and give each schedule its own **time zone**, for example Australia/Sydney, Europe/London and America/Toronto, all at 08:00.

The important part is the time zone field. New schedules default to UTC, and a schedule left on UTC does not follow daylight saving, so the local arrival time moves by an hour twice a year in every office that observes it. With a named time zone, CloudFlow follows that region's clock changes for you.

Two more tips: name the copies clearly ("Summary - Sydney"), and remember each run counts towards your monthly workflow runs, so three schedules use three runs per day.
